Warcraft 3D — How to play

  • Left click / drag: select units & buildings
  • Right click: move · harvest (peasant → tree/mine) · attack (→ enemy)
  • A + left click: attack-move · S: stop · H: hold
  • G: train Peasant · F: train Footman · B: build Farm (+6 food)
  • Camera: WASD / arrows · wheel zoom · middle-drag or Q/E rotate · edge pan
  • Peasants carry 10 gold/wood per trip back to the Town Hall.
  • Survive Orc waves. Destroy the Orc Stronghold to win.
